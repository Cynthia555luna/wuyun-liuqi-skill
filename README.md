### 📖 English
Wu Yun Liu Qi (Five Movements Six Qi) Skill

A comprehensive Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) analysis tool based on the ancient Five Movements Six Qi theory. This skill enables users to:

- Constitution Analysis : Calculate personal constitution based on birth year, revealing inherent physical tendencies and health predispositions
- Annual Qi Analysis : Analyze the energetic characteristics of any given year, understanding climate patterns and their influence
- Disease Prediction : Predict susceptible diseases according to luck cycles and seasonal changes
- Health Guidance : Receive personalized health maintenance suggestions tailored to individual constitution
Rooted in the "Huangdi Neijing," this tool embodies the holistic concept of "unity of man and nature," bridging ancient wisdom with modern wellness practices.

Enraizada en el "Huangdi Neijing", esta herramienta encarna el concepto holístico de "la unidad del hombre y la naturaleza", uniendo la sabiduría ancestral con prácticas de bienestar modernas.
